# Changelog

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-20
- **Quire on the clock.** With Quire 0.33 or newer on the same phone, the clock shows the focus timer while it runs,
  what is on now or comes next (with a countdown), what starts tomorrow, and how many tasks are due. Allow it once in
  Vesper's settings (Quire section); it is read on the phone only and never includes grades.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-20
- The notification list scrolls (up to 30 apps instead of 5).
- Notifications show the app's launcher icon.
- App names that Android hides from other apps (such as YouTube) are read correctly; if one still cannot be read, a readable name is made from the package.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-20
- First release: a bedside clock that opens when the phone starts charging.
- Big thin clock, date and battery on a black screen that shifts a few pixels now and then (OLED burn-in).
- Notifications of your other apps, one card per app, long-press to hide an app.
- Night tint, 24-hour or AM/PM, optional seconds, brightness, portrait and landscape layouts.
- Opens by itself on plug-in (display-over-apps or a full-screen alert), from a quick-settings tile, or from the app.
- Over-the-air updates from the GitHub releases of Quezka/vesper.
- English, Russian and Italian.
